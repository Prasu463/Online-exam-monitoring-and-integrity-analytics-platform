\# ExamGuard Project Documentation



\## 1. Project Title



Online Exam Monitoring and Integrity Analytics Platform



\## 2. Introduction



ExamGuard is an online examination monitoring and integrity analytics platform designed to support secure online examinations.



The system combines online examination functionality with candidate verification, monitoring, evidence collection, suspicious activity detection, and integrity analytics.



\## 3. Objectives



\- Provide an online examination platform.

\- Verify candidates before examinations.

\- Perform system and room checks.

\- Monitor candidates during examinations.

\- Detect suspicious activities.

\- Collect monitoring evidence.

\- Generate integrity analytics.

\- Provide faculty with monitoring and reporting features.

\## 6. System Workflow



1\. Candidate registers or logs in.

2\. Candidate verification is performed.

3\. Candidate completes the required system checks.

4\. Candidate performs room scanning and selfie capture.

5\. Candidate starts the examination.

6\. Monitoring mechanisms operate during the examination.

7\. Suspicious events can be recorded as evidence.

8\. Integrity analytics are generated.

9\. Faculty can review examination sessions and reports.

10\. Examination results are displayed.



\## 7. Expected Outcome



ExamGuard provides an integrated environment for conducting online examinations with candidate verification, monitoring, evidence management, and integrity analytics capabilities.
